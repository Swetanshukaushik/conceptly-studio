Product management, software and system design reels explain components, flows and trade-offs.
- Show systems as labelled boxes connected by arrows, with data or requests flowing along the arrows as `flow` objects. Reveal components in the order the narrator names them and keep a consistent colour per component type (client, service, database, queue, cache).
- For trade-offs, show two alternatives side by side with a `stat` or bar whose size comes from the numbers in the narration.
- For code, show a short snippet as a `formula` or text card of at most 6 short lines, only if the narration reads it out.
- Use the `explainer_window` layout with a two-line `header` and the `workshop_blue` look.
