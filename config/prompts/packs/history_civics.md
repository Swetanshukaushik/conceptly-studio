History and civics reels are stories about people, places and events.
- Open with a question or a moment in time, then move forward through events in order; show the date or period as a small `title` or `tag` when the narration states it.
- Build the set from `prop` prefabs (hall, building, humanoid, crowd, flag, document, book, lamp, podium) and stage the action. Stylised figures only: never imply a likeness of a real person.
- Use `image` objects with exact queries (proper nouns and years that appear in the narration) for real photographs, documents or places. Never request an image the narration does not mention.
- Use a timeline curve with markers when the beat is about sequence or cause and effect over years. State only facts, dates and numbers present in the narration.
- Prefer the `full` layout and the `warm_archive` look unless the topic is clearly modern.
